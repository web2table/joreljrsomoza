The platform

One platform.
Each restaurant, its own space.

Web2Table brings restaurant storefronts and operational workspaces together. Each restaurant has its own customer-facing storefront, while its team works in a dedicated environment. The platform is designed to support multiple restaurants without mixing their data and operations.

What Web2Table provides

Tools for everyday restaurant operations.

01

Online Storefront

Give customers a dedicated online place to browse a restaurant's menu and place orders.

02

Menu & Availability

Manage menu items and communicate product availability through the restaurant's storefront.

03

Order Management

Receive and manage customer orders through the restaurant's dedicated workspace.

04

Branch Management

Support restaurant businesses operating with a main branch and independently managed additional branches.

05

Kitchen Coordination

Make order details available to kitchen staff through the platform's kitchen ticket workflow.

06

Dedicated Restaurant Workspaces

Keep each restaurant's workspace, customer-facing storefront, and operational data separate within the multi-tenant platform.

How it works

From restaurant setup
to customer orders.

01

Restaurant gets its own space

A restaurant is set up on Web2Table with its own storefront and dedicated workspace.

02

Team manages its storefront and operations

The restaurant team manages its menu, product availability, branches, and incoming orders.

03

Customers place orders online

Customers visit the restaurant's storefront to view its menu and submit orders, which the restaurant team can manage in its workspace.

About Web2Table

Helping restaurants operate digitally.

Web2Table is being built to make online ordering and restaurant operations more organized and accessible. Our focus is a practical platform that connects each restaurant's online storefront with the tools its team uses to manage orders and daily activity.

Web2Table is a restaurant ordering platform. It is not presented as a point-of-sale system, and it does not provide invoicing or receipt issuance.
